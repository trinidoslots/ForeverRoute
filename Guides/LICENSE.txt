Changes made for ForeverRoute:
- Guides register through ForeverRoute.RegisterGuide instead of
  RXPGuides.RegisterGuide.
- The locale check at the top of the files uses ForeverRoute's flag, and a line
  ForeverRoute.guideFile = "..." after it names the file, so a translated guide can
  be matched with its English twin.
- enUS\forever\RestedXP-Skyborne.lua holds RestedXP's two Zephras Isle guides followed by the
  guides of RestedXP's Forever\Forever.lua (levels 13-60), registered the same way.
- The step text itself is unchanged. In the program (not in these files) a
  translated guide that is behind its English version is completed from the English
  guide: steps the translation does not have yet are shown in English.
- The translations (deDE, esES, frFR, ptBR, zhCN) are the ones RestedXP ships in
  lang\Guides-<locale>.

DungeonStats.lua, EmergencyData.lua and NpcNames.lua next to the program files hold RestedXP's
dungeon rating table and its list of emergency items and spells (DB\forever\db.lua:
addon.dungeonStats, addon.emergencyItems, addon.emergencySpells) and its table of creature
names per game language (locale\NPCnames.lua), copied
unchanged, under the same license. tools\Update-RxpData.js writes them.
